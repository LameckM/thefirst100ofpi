Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Number Stories does not ask you to sign up. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.

Saved in your browser

Your saved stories and drafts, personal words, memory palace, practice scores, review schedule, pi best and colour theme are stored in your own browser. They do not sync between devices. Clearing this site's data deletes them.

Sent to the server

To make words, stories and checks, the numbers and words you type for them are sent to our server. The app does not save them, but the server and hosting provider may record requests, including those numbers and words and your IP address, in their logs.

Do not enter passwords, PINs, card numbers or other secrets.

Questions

Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so leave out private details.
